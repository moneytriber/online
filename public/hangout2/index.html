Salary lands on the 25th, by the 5th you are asking where it went

Rent, fuel, school fees, the cousin who needs help, the aso ebi you could not say no to, every month there is a reason, and at the end of the year you have worked hard with very little to show for it.

You have heard people talk about T-bills, stocks and land, you have also watched friends lose money to apps that vanished overnight, so you either do nothing or you guess.

December is the month most people spend without thinking, this year spend one Saturday of it learning how to make your money work for you.

What you take home

Six things you take home from the event

  • The big one

    How to build wealth on purpose

    The real principles of wealth creation, how to grow your investment portfolio step by step, and a written 12 month wealth plan built at your table with your own income and your own goals.

  • The money market and how to spot good opportunities

    Whether you earn ₦150k or ₦1.5m a month, a clear way to start building wealth from your current level and still support family without going broke.

Before you go further

This event is not for everyone, that is on purpose

It is for you if

  • You earn steady money, from a salary or a business, but have little to show for it at year end
  • You want to start investing and have no idea which option suits you
  • Someone has pitched you a scheme that sounds too good and you want to know how to check
  • Your business is doing well but you own nothing outside it
  • You are ready to look at your own numbers honestly for one day

It is not for you if

  • You want to double your money in 30 days
  • You are hoping for a hot stock tip to get rich by January
  • You want motivation without doing any work on your own plan
  • You are not willing to talk about what you spend
